## Objectives

Industry 4.0 is the transformation of industrial processes through sensors, communication and computational processing. The course covers Cyber Physical Systems, the Internet of Things, cloud computing, machine learning and data analytics as the drivers of that change, and the Industrial IoT (IIoT) as the application of IoT in industry.

## Learning outcomes

1. Describe IoT systems, their architecture, characteristics and applications.
2. Explain the implications of limitations in embedded devices on the design and operation of networking protocols of an IoT system.
3. Design an IoT system that satisfies application requirements using appropriate networking technology.
4. Design an IIoT system, with awareness of the major aspects of building one, including fog computing and cybersecurity.
5. Awareness of the role of disruptive technologies such as RFID, digital twin, augmented reality and collaborative robots in smart factories.
6. Awareness of the role of Industry 5.0 for a sustainable environment.
